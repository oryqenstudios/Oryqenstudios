01

General Questions

Who does ORYQEN STUDIOS work with?

We work with Singapore businesses — especially SMEs, F&B brands, interior design firms and professional service providers.

What information do I need before starting a project?

A short description of your business, your goals for the website, any existing branding, and examples of websites you like. We help with the rest during discovery.

02

Website Design

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Yes. Every website is designed mobile-first and tested on phones, tablets and desktops.

Do you design custom websites or use templates?

We design each website for the specific business rather than reusing a template.

03

Pricing

How much does a website cost in Singapore?

Cost depends on the number of pages, content, features and integrations. We provide a quotation after understanding your requirements.

Are there additional costs besides design and development?

Possible additional costs include domain registration, hosting, third-party subscriptions, photography, copywriting and ongoing maintenance.

04

Project Timeline

How long does website development take?

Timelines depend on scope and how quickly content and feedback are provided. A schedule is agreed during planning.

05

Search Foundations

Is SEO included in website development?

Yes, on-page and technical SEO foundations are included: metadata, headings, URLs, sitemap, structured data and performance. Ongoing SEO campaigns are separate.

How do you prepare content for modern search?

We organise accurate content, descriptive headings and direct answers so search systems can understand your business. Placement in search results is never guaranteed.

06

Domain and Hosting

Do you provide domain and hosting assistance?

Yes. We help you connect a domain and set up hosting as part of launch.

07

Website Maintenance

Can I update my website after launch?

Yes. We can set up an update workflow for you, or handle updates through an optional maintenance arrangement.
