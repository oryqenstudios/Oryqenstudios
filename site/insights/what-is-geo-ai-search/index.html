What Is GEO and How Does Modern Search Affect Businesses?

A plain-language explanation of Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) and practical steps businesses can take as answer-led search grows.

What GEO means

Answer-led search tools summarise information from across the web into a direct answer. GEO focuses on making your content a reliable, well-structured source for those summaries.

How GEO relates to SEO

Traditional SEO helps pages rank in search results. GEO shares the same foundations — crawlable content, clear structure, accurate information — and adds emphasis on direct answers and consistent business identity.

Practical steps for businesses

These steps help both human readers and search systems:

  • Answer common customer questions directly and concisely
  • Use descriptive headings that match how people ask questions
  • Describe your services, service area and industries precisely
  • Keep business details consistent across your website and listings
  • Show authorship and publication dates on articles
  • Avoid exaggerated or unverifiable claims

What GEO cannot promise

No one controls how search tools select sources. GEO improves the clarity and usefulness of your content, but it cannot guarantee that your business will be mentioned in any search-generated answer.
