Workflow

How the project runs

A structured six-stage process keeps scope, feedback and timelines clear.

  1. Stage 1

    Discover

    We discuss your business, customers, goals and what the website needs to achieve.

  2. Stage 2

    Plan

    We define pages, features, content needs and integrations.

  3. Stage 3

    Design

    We create the visual direction and key page designs.

  4. Stage 4

    Develop

    We build the website with responsive layouts, SEO foundations and integrations.

  5. Stage 5

    Test and Refine

    We test across devices and browsers, check forms, speed and metadata.

  6. Stage 6

    Launch and Handover

    We connect your domain, publish the site and confirm everything works.

FAQ

Common questions

Will a redesign hurt my Google rankings?

Any redesign carries some risk. We reduce it by mapping old URLs to new ones, setting redirects and preserving important content.

Can you reuse my existing content?

Yes. We review what you have, keep what works, and recommend improvements where content is outdated or unclear.

Do I need a new domain?

Usually not. Most redesigns keep the existing domain.
