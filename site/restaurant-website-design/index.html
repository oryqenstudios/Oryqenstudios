Workflow

How the project runs

A structured six-stage process keeps scope, feedback and timelines clear.

  1. Stage 1

    Discover

    We discuss your business, customers, goals and what the website needs to achieve.

  2. Stage 2

    Plan

    We define pages, features, content needs and integrations.

  3. Stage 3

    Design

    We create the visual direction and key page designs.

  4. Stage 4

    Develop

    We build the website with responsive layouts, SEO foundations and integrations.

  5. Stage 5

    Test and Refine

    We test across devices and browsers, check forms, speed and metadata.

  6. Stage 6

    Launch and Handover

    We connect your domain, publish the site and confirm everything works.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I update my menu myself?

Yes. Menus can be structured so you can update items and prices without technical help.

Can you connect my existing reservation system?

In most cases, yes — we embed or link to the booking platform you already use.

Do I still need a website if I'm on delivery apps?

Delivery apps show your listing among competitors. Your own website presents your brand, story and full offering on your terms.
