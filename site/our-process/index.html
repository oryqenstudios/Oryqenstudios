Our process

A clear path from first call to launch

Six stages, each with defined inputs and deliverables, so you always know what happens next.

01Discover02Plan03Design04Develop05Test and Refine06Launch and Handover
  1. 01

    Discover

    What happens
    We discuss your business, customers, goals and what the website needs to achieve.
    You provide
    Background on your business, current website (if any) and examples you like.
    We deliver
    A summary of goals and a recommended approach.
    Next step
    We confirm scope and provide a quotation.
  2. 02

    Plan

    What happens
    We define pages, features, content needs and integrations.
    You provide
    Content, service details, branding assets and any required integrations.
    We deliver
    A sitemap, page outline and project timeline.
    Next step
    Design direction begins once the plan is approved.
  3. 03

    Design

    What happens
    We create the visual direction and key page designs.
    You provide
    Feedback on the design direction.
    We deliver
    Desktop and mobile designs for key pages.
    Next step
    Approved designs move into development.
  4. 04

    Develop

    What happens
    We build the website with responsive layouts, SEO foundations and integrations.
    You provide
    Final content and timely answers to questions.
    We deliver
    A working staging website for review.
    Next step
    Testing and refinement.
  5. 05

    Test and Refine

    What happens
    We test across devices and browsers, check forms, speed and metadata.
    You provide
    A review of the staging site and a consolidated list of changes.
    We deliver
    Refinements and a final pre-launch checklist.
    Next step
    Launch scheduling.
  6. 06

    Launch and Handover

    What happens
    We connect your domain, publish the site and confirm everything works.
    You provide
    Domain access or DNS assistance from your provider.
    We deliver
    A live website, handover notes and guidance on updates.
    Next step
    Optional ongoing maintenance and support.
