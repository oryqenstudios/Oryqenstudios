FAQ

Frequently asked questions

All FAQs
How much does a website cost in Singapore?

Cost depends on the number of pages, content, features and integrations. We provide a quotation after understanding your requirements.

How long does website development take?

Timelines depend on scope and how quickly content and feedback are provided. A schedule is agreed during planning.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Yes. Every website is designed mobile-first and tested on phones, tablets and desktops.

Is SEO included in website development?

Yes, on-page and technical SEO foundations are included: metadata, headings, URLs, sitemap, structured data and performance. Ongoing SEO campaigns are separate.
