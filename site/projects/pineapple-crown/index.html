Interactive · Interactive Product Launch Website

Pineapple Crown Pro

An interactive website concept for a fictional phone launch, built so visitors explore the product by scrolling, dragging and taking it apart rather than reading a specification sheet.

01

Business objective

The objective of this concept was to explore how a product launch page could let visitors handle a device on screen. The website needed to turn design, finishes and internal construction into interactions that invite people to keep exploring.

02

Design challenges

  • Making scroll and drag interactions feel natural rather than distracting
  • Presenting five finishes without repeating the same view
  • Explaining internal construction without a dense specification table
  • Keeping a dark, premium presentation readable
  • Presenting fictional branding and illustrative prices honestly
03

Proposed solution

The concept opens with a phone that spins through its finishes as visitors scroll, then moves through lighting sequences on the camera and back glass, a finish selector and an exploded view of ten layers that visitors can pull apart themselves. A short set of key numbers and a buy call to action close the journey.

04

Why the theme fits the service

The pineapple theme gives the product a distinctive identity: spiral patterns, rind textures and finishes named Ripen, Rind, Golden Flesh, Crown Green and Core. The dark background keeps attention on the device and its interactions.

05

Development approach

The concept was developed as a scroll-driven product experience with section navigation, interactive finish controls and a hands-on exploded view. Navigation, section order and the interactive sections were reviewed on the live concept website.

Concept Status

Concept Project — an independent, fictional product-launch website concept. Pineapple and Crown Pro are invented concept branding, and the specifications and prices shown are illustrative. No client, sales or performance results are being claimed.
