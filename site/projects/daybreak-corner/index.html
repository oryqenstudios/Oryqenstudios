F&B · Café / F&B Website

Daybreak Corner — Singapore Café Website Concept

A café website concept designed for a Singapore neighbourhood café, featuring a welcoming visual style and an easy-to-browse menu.

View Live ConceptConcept Project
Classification
Independent Concept
Industry
F&B
Website type
Café / F&B Website
Design direction
  • Warm neutral palette
  • Serif café branding
  • Food and drink photography
  • Simple menu layouts
Key concept features
  • Dedicated food-and-drink menu page
  • Coffee & Tea, Toast & Eggs and Bakes categories
  • Menu category jump links
  • Item descriptions and illustrative SGD prices
  • Home, Menu and Visit navigation
  • Phone-friendly menu presentation
01

Business objective

Present a fictional Singapore neighbourhood café through a welcoming website where visitors can explore its food and drinks. The live concept introduces Daybreak Corner as a Tiong Bahru café concept centred on coffee, kaya toast and slow mornings.

02

Design challenges

  • Keeping café branding, menu categories and item details readable on a phone
  • Balancing food-and-drink photography with a straightforward menu
  • Making illustrative business information clearly distinguishable from real café details
03

Proposed solution

The concept uses a dedicated menu page with Coffee & Tea, Toast & Eggs and Bakes sections. Category links lead to the relevant menu section, while descriptions and illustrative SGD prices sit alongside the food and drink listings. Home and Visit pages complete the browsing journey.

04

Why the theme fits the service

The warm neutral palette, serif branding and familiar café photography support the concept’s neighbourhood identity. Spacious menu rows and clear category headings keep practical information easy to browse.

05

Development approach

The portfolio cover was captured directly from the live menu page at a phone-sized viewport. Two adjoining sections of that screenshot are presented side by side to preserve the café branding, category links and drink listings within the portfolio’s existing image proportions. The website’s Home, Menu and Visit navigation and its mobile menu were reviewed for this case study.

Concept Status

Concept Project — an independent, fictional café website concept, not a live client project or a real café. The website labels its menu prices and opening hours as illustrative. No client or performance results are being claimed.
