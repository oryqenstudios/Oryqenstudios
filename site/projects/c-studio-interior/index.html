Interior Design · Portfolio and Enquiry Website

C Studio Interior

A warm, photography-led website concept for a Singapore interior design studio, designed to present residential spaces, communicate a thoughtful design process and encourage homeowners to begin a conversation.

01

Business objective

The objective of this concept was to explore how a Singapore interior design studio could present its work in a calm, professional and highly visual way. The website needed to help homeowners understand the studio’s design philosophy, explore its residential spaces and find a clear path towards discussing their renovation requirements.

02

Design challenges

  • Allowing interior photography and materials to remain the primary visual focus
  • Creating a premium identity without making the interface feel cold or unapproachable
  • Explaining the studio’s proposed design process using concise and understandable content
  • Balancing inspiration with a clear homeowner enquiry journey
  • Presenting placeholder content honestly while project information awaits confirmation
  • Maintaining a refined experience across desktop and mobile screens
03

Proposed solution

The proposed solution uses large interior imagery, warm neutral colours, natural wood tones, spacious layouts and elegant serif typography. The website is organised around the studio’s spaces, working approach, introduction and contact journey, allowing visitors to move naturally from visual inspiration towards starting a conversation.

04

Why the theme fits the service

Interior design is experienced through space, material, light and atmosphere. A warm editorial direction allows the studio’s visual work to lead while the restrained colour palette and typography communicate care, refinement and attention to detail.

05

Development approach

The concept was developed as a responsive, photography-led website with clear navigation, reusable content sections and layouts adapted for mobile, tablet and desktop viewing. Image presentation, readable typography, enquiry visibility and consistent spacing were prioritised throughout the experience.

Concept Status

This is an independent concept created to demonstrate ORYQEN STUDIOS’ proposed approach to an interior design portfolio website. Project names, descriptions, imagery and business content remain subject to the studio owner’s review and approval. No client or performance results are being claimed.
